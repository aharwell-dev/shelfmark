Shelfmark

Interactive prototype of the iPhone app, using sample data. Everything is clickable.

Catalog: search or sort, then tap a book and choose “Show on shelf.”

Scan: choose a bookcase and a shelf, tap Camera, then the shutter. This runs a simulated recognition pass and opens the review screen.

Finder: try searching jacobs, koolhaas or NA2500. Switch between Plan and Photo.

Titles, call numbers and colors are illustrative.
